Where the paid services are better

Nothing to run yourself

A cloud service has no server to install, update or back up. With Narancs Signage that is a small Ubuntu machine or a Docker container on your side.

Support with a contract

Paid plans come with a help desk and guarantees. Here you get the documentation and GitHub issues.

Native smart-TV apps

Some vendors offer apps for Samsung Tizen or LG webOS. Narancs Signage runs in the TV's browser or on a small player box.
